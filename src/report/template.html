What to try

    Wi-Fi check

    Router ping during each test (one change at a time, everything restored afterwards). A freeze = ping above ms or lost.

    Marked lag spikes

    Each card judges the 4 s before the key press (falling back to 10 s, with low confidence). Click a card to zoom every chart to it.

    Findings

      Timeline

      Drag on a chart to zoom; double-click to reset. Red lines = your marks, orange = game stalls ≥100 ms, grey = outside match play.

      Drivers

      Longest DPC / interrupt-handler run per driver. Under 0.5 ms is fine, 1-2 ms can cause small hitches, over 2 ms delays packets, frames and audio. Graphics drivers routinely reach 1-2 ms while gaming; they are judged from 2 ms.
